# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Structile a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward other community members. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the verified GitHub profile channel. The owner will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may remove content or participation privileges when necessary.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1. The full reference text is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
